Privacy Policy & Security

Your hydration rhythm belongs solely to you.

SipStreak is crafted as a quiet, respectful wellness companion. We believe your daily health habits are personal, intimate, and deserve absolute privacy by design.

100% Local-First No Cloud Accounts Zero Ads or Trackers Instant One-Tap Reset
1. Data Architecture

Local-First On-Device Storage

Everything stays on your phone. Nothing touches our servers.

All water intake logs, hydration volume records, custom goals, active streaks, reminder schedules, and appearance preferences are stored exclusively on your device using Android local storage (Room database and Jetpack DataStore).

  • No account required: You never need to provide your name, phone number, email address, or create a password to use SipStreak.
  • No remote database: SipStreak does not operate remote servers to host or sync your personal hydration history.
  • Works completely offline: SipStreak operates flawlessly without an active internet connection.

Because your data is preserved purely on your local hardware, uninstalling the app or wiping app storage in Android settings permanently deletes your records, unless you use your device's native Android Google Drive backup.

2. Device Permissions

Strictly Necessary Permissions

Only what is required to provide timely hydration nudges.

SipStreak requests the bare minimum permissions necessary to deliver gentle hydration reminders at your chosen times. We never request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or external media access.

Post Notifications Optional

POST_NOTIFICATIONS is requested only when you choose to enable hydration reminders. It is used solely to deliver quiet notifications on your device.

Boot Receiver System

RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED allows the Android system to reschedule your existing reminder alarms after your device is restarted or powered back on.

3. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

Zero Ads, Zero Trackers

Your attention and health habits are not for sale.

SipStreak contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no behavioral profiling, and no commercial tracking libraries. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your hydration entries to data brokers, insurers, or marketers.

Your daily sips, wake-up times, and streak lengths remain confidential on your device.

4. Your Control & Data Erasure

Complete Data Control

Erase your entire history with a single tap at any time.

You have full control over your data directly within the app:

  • Edit or Delete Individual Logs: Tap any recorded entry on the Today screen or History journal to modify the amount or remove it completely.
  • Clear Intake Logs: Erase all historical consumption records while keeping your preferences intact.
  • Reset Everything: In Settings → General → Reset all data, you can instantly erase all logs, goal history, and custom settings, cancelling all alarms and restoring SipStreak to its initial clean state.
5. Health & Children's Privacy

Wellness Purpose & Protection

Important guidance for healthy hydration habits.

General Wellness Purpose: SipStreak is a habit-building lifestyle and logging tool for everyday hydration. It is not a medical device and does not offer medical diagnoses, prescriptions, or clinical fluid management advice. Consult a medical professional regarding personal fluid intake requirements.

Children's Privacy: SipStreak does not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). Because no personal data is transmitted off the device, children can use the logging features safely under parental guidance.
